[h1]What afk_farm does each cycle[/h1]
[olist]
[*] Checks power (after you own [b]Sunflowers[/b])
[*] Buys unlocks it can afford with [b]unlock()[/b]
[*] Farms the field (1 pass, or 3 after you own [b]Polyculture[/b])
[*] Runs dinosaurs when you own [b]Dinosaurs[/b] + [b]Hats[/b] and have food ready
[*] Runs mazes when you own [b]Mazes[/b] and have Weird Substance ready
[/olist]

Missing features are skipped until unlocked. You do not need to rewrite windows for each new system.

[h1]What unlocks turn on inside afk_farm[/h1]
[list]
[*] [b]Watering[/b]: waters tiles under half full (faster growth)
[*] [b]Pumpkins[/b]: solid pumpkin square, replants [b]Dead_Pumpkin[/b], waits for giant, harvests at origin
[*] [b]Cactus[/b]: grows, sorts, then harvests the cactus square
[*] [b]Sunflowers[/b]: north sunflower strip + automatic power restock
[*] [b]Polyculture[/b]: farms 3 times per cycle
[*] [b]Megafarm[/b]: uses extra drones to go faster (until then, everything runs on one drone)
[*] [b]Hats[/b] + [b]Dinosaurs[/b]: bone runs (can shrink to 8x8 with [b]Debug_2[/b], then restore)
[*] [b]Mazes[/b]: Weird Substance maze treasure runs
[/list]

[h1]Late farm layout[/h1]
[list]
[*] Southwest: pumpkin square ([b]6x6[/b] on [b]8x8[/b]/[b]12x12[/b]; half-field on bigger farms)
[*] Southeast: cactus square (same size)
[*] Top rows: sunflowers (after unlock)
[*] Free tiles: hay-heavy feed mix; sparse trees; carrots only when hay/wood stocks are healthy
[*] If hay or wood runs low, free tiles refill that resource before planting more carrots
[/list]

[h1]Normal things that look scary[/h1]
[list]
[*] The whole farm becomes sunflowers, then returns to normal.
Power restock ([b]Sunflowers[/b]).

[*] The farm shrinks during dinosaur mode, then returns.
Fast 8x8 dino clear ([b]Debug_2[/b]).

[*] Early [b]afk_farm[/b] ignores mazes or dinosaurs.
It is waiting for [b]Mazes[/b] / [b]Dinosaurs[/b] / [b]Hats[/b] and the matching resources.

[*] It never stops alone.
Stop the code window when you want control back.
[/list]
