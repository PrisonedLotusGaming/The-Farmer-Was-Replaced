Buy in this order. First unlock prices shown. Prefer [b]Speed[/b] ranks whenever they are cheap. Buy [b]Watering[/b] early (about [b]50 wood[/b]) so crops grow faster.



[b]Plant costs:[/b] hover [b]plant()[/b] for your amounts. Carrots spend hay + wood. Pumpkins spend carrots. Crop upgrades raise those costs. Starters keep feed crops on other tiles so you do not need a separate stockpile grind. Pumpkins also need a [b]solid square[/b] (up to [b]6x6[/b]) for giant yield; feed crops stay outside that square.



[b]Expand ranks:[/b] Farm size jumps when you buy Expand (not +1 each time). Known early ranks:
[list]
[*] 1st: [b]1[/b] -> [b]1x3[/b]
[*] 2nd: [b]1x3[/b] -> [b]3x3[/b]
[*] Later ranks keep jumping (example: [b]8x8[/b] -> [b]12x12[/b]). Check the unlock tree for the next size.
[/list]
